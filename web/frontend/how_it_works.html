The AI Pipeline

Understanding the Neural Architecture from raw signal to diagnosis.

1
📡

1. Data Acquisition

The process begins with an EEG headset using 14 sensors placed gently on the scalp. These sensors detect the natural electrical activity produced by your neurons communicating. This biological information is immediately converted into digital data that our system can process.

🧠 Brain Activity ➝ ⚡ Sensors ➝ 🔢 Digital Input
2
🧹

2. Preprocessing (Filtering)

Raw EEG data often contains "noise" from muscle movement, blinking, or nearby electronics. We apply a specific filter (1-40Hz) to remove these distractions, keeping only the clear brainwaves—Delta, Theta, Alpha, and Beta—that are essential for assessing mental fatigue.

〰️ Noisy Signal ➝ 🎛️ Noise Removal ➝ 📉 Clean Waves
3
🧊

3. Data Structuring

To help the AI analyze the continuous stream of brain activity, we organize the data into 4-second segments. These segments are structured into a 3D format that captures three layers of information at once: where in the brain activity is happening, what frequencies are active, and how they change over time.

Channels × Frequency × Time
4
💎

4. Feature Extraction

Our neural network processes this complex data to extract the most meaningful patterns. It compresses the information into a concise summary vector. This step is crucial because it isolates the specific patterns of the strong>mental state itself, ensuring the analysis focuses on the condition rather than the individual's unique biological traits.

🧊 Raw Data ➝ 🤖 Analysis ➝ 🆔 State Vector
5
📏

5. Comparative Diagnosis

Rather than needing thousands of examples to learn, our system compares the new patient's data directly to established reference profiles (prototypes) of "Relaxed" and "Burnout" states. It measures how mathematically similar the new data is to these references and classifies the condition based on proximity.

📍 Patient Data ↔️ Reference Profiles
6
🧠

6. Transparent Results (XAI)

Trust is key in medical AI. Instead of just providing a diagnosis, our system uses Explainable AI to generate a visual heatmap. This map shows doctors exactly which brain regions and wave patterns influenced the decision, allowing for a transparent and verifiable diagnosis.

✅ Result + 🔥 Visual Evidence
